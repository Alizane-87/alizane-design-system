    Under the hood

    How the AI works

    1

    Image model

    A Teachable Machine model trained on labelled photos of four house types. It runs in the browser with TensorFlow.js, so photos are never uploaded. Below 60% confidence, the app asks you to choose the type yourself.

    2

    Hazard data

    Each state has a level from 1 (low) to 4 (very high) for five hazards, taken from the BIS seismic zone map (IS 1893), the BMTPC Vulnerability Atlas, NDMA, IMD and GSI landslide maps.

    3

    Risk score

    House type and your answers set a vulnerability from 0 to 1 for each hazard. The score combines it with the hazard level:

    Risk = 100 × H × (0.25 + 0.75 × V)

    H for levels 1–4: 0.20, 0.45, 0.70, 0.95

    What it cannot do

    It cannot see inside walls, test materials or check foundations. The hazard data is per state, not per street. A photo of only one side of a house can mislead the model. Treat the score as a reason to act and to ask an engineer, never as proof that a house is safe.
